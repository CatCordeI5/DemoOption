"If I were asked when someone or I might finally feel free or happy, here is my answer: you will realize that you never actually ran out of time; you simply spent it overthinking, planning too far ahead, and doubting yourself, leaving everything for "later." And when that "later" never arrives, you finally realize that what you had been putting off wasn't just a plan, but your own life. So, perhaps the question isn't about **when** one might feel free or happy, but rather: why not start living that way right now?"
